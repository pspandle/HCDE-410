# A1 — Working with Open Data

**Dataset:** Burke-Gilman Trail counter north of NE 70th St  
**Source:** https://data.seattle.gov/Transportation/Burke-Gilman-Trail-north-of-NE-70th-St-Bike-and-Pe/2z5v-ecg8 (pulled 2026-10-10)  
**License / terms of use:** Public Domain, according to the dataset metadata on the City of Seattle Open Data portal.

## Questions asked
- When is the trail busiest and quietest?
- How do cyclists and pedestrians use the trail differently throughout the day?
- Is the trail used more for commuting or recreation?
- Does the direction of travel suggest commuting patterns?

## How to re-run
Open `A1.ipynb` and run all cells (needs `requests`, `pandas`, `matplotlib`).
